Cooking Steps

01

Prepare ingredients

Prepare beef, egg, carrot, cooked rice and seasonings.

02

Dice carrot

Wash carrot and cut into small cubes.

03

Slice beef

Cut beef into thin strips.

04

Make seasoning sauce

Mix soy sauce, oyster sauce and pepper.

05

Marinate beef

Coat beef with sauce and leave to marinate.

06

Scramble eggs

Heat pan, fry beaten eggs and set aside.

07

Stir fry carrot

Add diced carrot into pan and stir fry briefly.

08

Add rice & beef

Add rice, beef and egg. Stir well and serve.
