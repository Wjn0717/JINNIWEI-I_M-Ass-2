Cooking Steps

01

Peel all vegetables

Peel carrot, potato and pumpkin.

02

Dice vegetables

Wash vegetables and cut into small cubes.

03

Slice beef

Cut beef into thin strips.

04

Make seasoning sauce

Mix soy sauce, oyster sauce and chilli powder and black pepper.

05

Marinate beef

Coat beef with sauce and leave to marinate.

06

Scramble eggs & add rice

Heat pan, fry beaten eggs, add cooked rice and mix well.

07

Stir fry vegetables

Add diced vegetables into pan and stir fry briefly.

08

Cook beef & finish

Add beef, stir-fry on high heat. Garnish with spring onion and serve.

About Beef & Vegetable Fried Rice

This simple homemade fried rice uses marinated beef, carrot and potato. It’s a quick and easy meal to make at home.
